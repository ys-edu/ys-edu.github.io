The developers of Glaze analyzed the AI models that train on online images,
then used a set of machine learning algorithms to edit submitted images
in a way that the AI models can detect, but appears unedited to human eyes.

There are some limitations to Glaze - simpler art styles are more visually
affected by Glaze's edits, and there have been attempts by AI models to undo
edits on Glazed images. The system will need to be updated often in order to
account for AI model updates.

"What Is Glaze?" article.

A particular quote from Melissa Heikkilä's article about the Glaze Project:

"In replies from more than 1,200 artists—far more than the average number of responses to user studies in computer science—the team found that the vast majority of creators had read about art being used to train models, and 97% expected AI to decrease some artists’ job security. A quarter said AI art had already affected their jobs."
